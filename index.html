I'm a sophomore at Stanford studying Symbolic Systems (AI track) with a minor in Gender Studies. I grew up in Brazil's Pantanal region in a Japanese-Brazilian community, which means I think constantly about what it means to sit between worlds, cultures, and systems.

My work lives at the intersection of AI, inequality, and entrepreneurship. I do research in Professor Lukas Althoff's lab on automated multi-agent pipelines and global inequality data. I've built products for women in Brazil's informal economy. I've done optimization modeling for the Galapagos Islands. I'm always building something.

Currently: Product at Selleb in New York City, and incoming research intern at Mitsubishi Research Institute in Tokyo this summer.

02 / Research

Work that asks harder questions.

01
Multi-Agent Systems · Policy
Automated Pipelines for Global Inequality Data
Building automated multi-agent pipelines to collect and structure a global database of high school STEM graduation requirements. Firecrawl-based web scraping with careful schema design around governance levels and data confidence. Supervised by Professor Lukas Althoff.
02
NLP · Computational Social Science
Racialized Discourse & Symbolic Power in NYT Comments
Computational analysis of ~2 million New York Times comments on how "neutral" editorial framing legitimizes racialized discourse about Latinos. Draws on Bourdieu's symbolic power framework, combining NLP methods with critical discourse analysis.
03
Economic-Ecological Modeling · Policy
60-Year Socio-Ecological Model of the Galápagos
Long-term economic–ecological trade-offs of tourism in the Galápagos Islands using machine learning–adjacent data science methods and optimization modeling to estimate a welfare-maximizing visitation threshold.
04
NLP · Social Theory
Classification of Gendered Linguistic Patterns in Digital Self-Presentation
End-to-end analysis pipeline combining NLP feature engineering, topic modeling, sentiment and emotion lexicons, and a supervised classifier that predicts gender-coded linguistic patterns at the sentence level.
05
SWE · Mobile Development
LinkedIn Spark
AI-powered feature on LinkedIn to spark meaningful coffee chats on the platform, blending gamification and AI to help users build more intentional, genuine connections.
