overview

LinkedIn Spark is an AI-powered feature built to spark meaningful coffee chats on the platform. The idea: most LinkedIn connections go nowhere. People follow each other but rarely actually talk. Spark blends gamification and AI to help users build more intentional, genuine professional connections, nudging passive networks into real conversations. Built and pitched at LinkedIn’s 2025 Sophomore Hackathon, where our team won.
